# Work From Home Policy



## Eligibility



Employees who have completed their probation period may request to work from home.



## Weekly Work From Home



Eligible employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.



The specific days should be agreed upon with the employee's manager.



## Temporary Remote Work



Employees may request temporary full-time remote work for health, family, or other exceptional circumstances.



Such requests require manager approval.



## Office Attendance



Employees are expected to attend important meetings, team events, and other activities that require in-person participation.



Managers may request additional office attendance when business requirements demand it.



## Equipment



Employees working from home may request a company laptop and other necessary equipment through the IT support process.
