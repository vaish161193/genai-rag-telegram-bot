# Leave Policy



## Annual Leave



Employees receive 24 days of annual leave per calendar year.



Annual leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days in advance.



## Sick Leave



Employees can take up to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.



For sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days, employees should provide appropriate medical documentation.



## Public Holidays



The company observes all public holidays announced for the employee's work location.



## Leave Approval



Leave requests should be submitted through the company's leave management system.



Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests.
