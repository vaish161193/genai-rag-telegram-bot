# Frequently Asked Questions



## Password Reset



Employees who forget their company password should use the password reset option on the company login page.



If the reset option does not work, contact the IT support team.



## IT Support



IT support is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.



Employees can contact IT support through the internal support portal.



## Joining the Company



New employees should complete their onboarding activities during their first week.



The onboarding process includes completing required documents, attending orientation, and setting up company accounts.



## Training



Employees can access approved internal training programs through the learning portal.



Managers can recommend additional training based on role requirements.



## Expense Reimbursement



Employees should submit eligible business expenses through the company's expense management system.



Receipts should be attached to the expense submission.
