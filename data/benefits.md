# Employee Benefits



## Health Insurance



Full-time employees receive health insurance coverage after completing 30 days of employment.



The health plan includes medical, dental, and vision coverage.



## Retirement Plan



Employees can participate in the company retirement savings plan.



The company provides a matching contribution of up to 5% of the employee's eligible salary.



## Learning and Development



Employees receive an annual learning and development allowance of $1,000.



This allowance can be used for approved courses, certifications, and professional training.



## Wellness Program



Employees can participate in the company wellness program.



The program may include fitness activities, wellness workshops, and health awareness sessions.
